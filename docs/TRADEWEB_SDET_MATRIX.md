## Career requirements and AI boundary

Q1 concerns education/equivalent practice; Q2 requests at least seven years' QA/testing experience; Q3, Q10–11 concern role history, problem solving and communication. A project cannot create years of employment or certify seniority. Use concrete, truthful examples from your own work alongside TradeFlow. R10 encourages AI-assisted development productivity; that is separate from any interview rule restricting live AI use. Practice explaining and editing the code independently.

## Decision

The actionable project gaps were reproducible institutional-demo setup, balanced timed scripts, a source/test mapping, explicit surface boundaries, independent study material and bounded executable rehearsal evidence. These are appropriate to close now. A new live FIX engine, Jenkins/GitLab installation, live feed, broker adapter or production financial rollout is unnecessary for this demonstration and would require separate justification/authorization. Readiness also requires the owner to answer the study guide and perform the walkthrough without assistance; CI cannot certify that personal skill.
